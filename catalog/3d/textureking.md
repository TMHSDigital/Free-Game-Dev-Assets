# TextureKing

Free stock photo textures (grunge, concrete, plaster, rust, wood, stone, metal and more), downloaded one JPG at a time. These are raw photographs, not seamless PBR sets. The Terms of Use page exists and allows commercial and personal use, but its redistribution clause reads against shipping the files inside a game, so this entry stays `needs-review` with `commercial: unknown`.

## Notes

- Grant: "any commercial or personal use"; you may modify the textures and use them in as many projects as you need, including client work
- Forbidden: resale or redistribution of the textures "modified, unmodified, standalone or combined with another product" without written (email) consent; sub-licensing or transferring the licence; claiming ownership; copyrighting a texture or a derivative of one
- The open question: a game build redistributes its textures, combined with another product. The terms do not name games, software or compiled binaries, and the clause as written covers that case unless TextureKing consents. The terms give info@reh3.com for licence questions. Get written permission before shipping, or use [texturecan](texturecan.md) or [ambientcg](ambientcg.md), which are CC0
- No credit clause anywhere in the terms, hence `attribution_required: false`
- Photos can show brands or logos; the page disclaims those trademarks
