# Kenney City Kit (Industrial)

25 CC0 modular factories and warehouses (v1.0, 2025). Original Kenney work. This is not a GTA V industrial extract and not a FiveM drop-in ydr pack.

- Live pack page states Creative Commons CC0 and 25 files. It does not list interchange formats; `formats` matches other Kenney 3D kits in this catalog.
- CC0 lets you re-export to other engines. That path still does not license Rockstar assets. See [`docs/fivem.md`](../../docs/fivem.md).
- Different pack from Kenney's older Industrial Kit mentioned on the hub card. This URL is the City series factory/warehouse set.
