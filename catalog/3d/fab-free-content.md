# Fab Free Listings and Limited-Time Free

Fab, Epic's asset marketplace, has two kinds of free content. Permanently free listings are found with the search page's Free filter and come from Epic, Quixel and third-party sellers. The Limited-Time Free program makes a few normally paid products free for two weeks, with a new set every two weeks; the current set is on the [Limited-Time Free](https://www.fab.com/limited-time-free) page. This entry points at both; individual giveaway items are not catalogued here because they change every fortnight.

## Notes

- Licence varies per listing: most carry the Fab Standard License, some carry CC-BY (credit required), and some Epic and Unreal-specific listings add a "UE-Only Content" line that restricts them to Unreal Engine. Read the Details panel and description of each listing before you rely on it
- Standard License listings: commercial use allowed, no credit required, not limited to Unreal unless the listing says UE-Only
- No redistribution for Standard License listings: the Fab EULA summary says you may not "Resell or redistribute the asset for free on a standalone basis or allow others to do the same". Do not commit Fab downloads in source form to a public repository
- Limited-Time Free items must be claimed inside the two-week window; the free price ends on the stated date (for example "Until October 20 at 9:59 AM ET" on 2026-10-13)
- Many free listings are Unreal-only by format even when the licence is not, because they ship as Unreal projects or asset packs
- Epic's own free packs are catalogued separately: [megascans-starter-content](megascans-starter-content.md), [electric-dreams-environment](../environment/electric-dreams-environment.md), [paragon-characters](../characters/paragon-characters.md)
- The entry `url` is a Fab search with the free filter on, because Fab has no stable "free listings" page. If Fab changes its search parameters the link breaks; open fab.com and filter by price "Free" instead
