# Megascans Starter Content (Free on Fab)

The permanently free slice of Quixel Megascans on Fab. Free claiming of the whole Megascans library ended at the end of 2024; from 2 January 2025 Epic kept a starter set free instead, announced as 1500+ assets, 5 tree packs and 11 scenes, under the Fab Standard License. The individual assets can be used in any engine or tool; the tree packs and scenes are Unreal Engine only. The live list is the Quixel Megascans seller page on Fab filtered to free, which also carries later free Quixel releases such as the Military Trench sample. For paid or previously claimed Megascans, see [fab-megascans-standard](fab-megascans-standard.md).

## Notes

- Mixed engine scope, so no `engine_lock`: Epic's announcement says the assets "can be used in any engine or tool", but the 5 tree packs and 11 scenes are "(Unreal Engine only)". Check each listing before using it outside Unreal
- If you claimed all Megascans on Fab before the end of 2024 you already own everything in the starter set, per the same announcement
- The Epic Content License's Megascans Addendum still has a "Free Assets" clause limiting Megascans acquired from Epic outside a Megascans plan to "internal evaluation purposes". That clause governs Megascans under the Epic Content License (the older Bridge and Quixel route); Epic's announcement and the Fab listings put the starter content under the Fab Standard License instead. Re-check if Epic changes either page
- No redistribution: the Fab EULA summary says you may not "Resell or redistribute the asset for free on a standalone basis or allow others to do the same". Do not commit Megascans source files to a public repository
- Prefer [poly-haven](../environment/poly-haven.md) or [ambientcg](ambientcg.md) when you need CC0 scans you can redistribute
- The entry `url` is the Quixel Megascans seller page with Fab's free-price filter in the query string. If Fab changes its filter parameters the link falls back to the full seller page (`fab.com/sellers/Quixel Megascans`, checked 2026-10-22); filter by price "Free" there
