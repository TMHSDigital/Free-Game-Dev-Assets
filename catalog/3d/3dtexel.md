# 3DTexel

Handmade CC0 library of about 1,650 assets: roughly 1,080 seamless PBR materials in 4K (some up to 8K), about 240 of them stylized or hand-painted, plus 49 HDRIs (up to 16K), 286 decals, 130 IES light profiles, 61 3D models, 18 vegetation atlases and 26 heightmaps and backdrops. The same site also sells AI generation credits and premium assets under a separate 3DTexel License; this entry covers only the free CC0 library.

## Notes

- Free to download, but a free account (login) is required
- License hub: [3dtexel.com/asset-license/](https://3dtexel.com/asset-license/). Free handcrafted assets are CC0; paid premium and AI-generated assets use the custom 3DTexel License (credit required, no resale of the raw files). Check the badge on each product page
- PBR sets ship base color, normal, roughness and height maps as TIFF or TGA; HDRIs ship as HDR and EXR
- Free Unreal Engine and Substance Painter plugins can browse and import the library
- The decal subset already has its own entry: [3dtexel-decals](../shaders-vfx/3dtexel-decals.md)
- Disclosure: submitted by the creator of 3DTexel
