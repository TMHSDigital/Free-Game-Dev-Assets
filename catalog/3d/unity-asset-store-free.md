# Unity Asset Store (free assets)

The free assets on Unity's Asset Store: models, textures, audio, tools and templates, most under Unity's standard Asset Store EULA and some under their publisher's own licence.

## Notes

- The url is a search with the free filter on; if Unity changes its search parameters, open the Asset Store and filter by price.
- Per item: read the licence named on each asset's page; most use the standard Asset Store EULA, and some publishers name their own. Asset Store assets may not be redistributed or resold as assets.
- Source: added for #100 (catalog coverage gaps); the licence was read at the source on 2026-11-18.

## Evidence

- Live [Asset Store Terms of Service and EULA](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms) (2026-11-18): "...will be subject to Unity's standard Unity Asset Store End User License Agreement (" EULA "), which is Appendix 1 to these Terms"
- Same page (2026-11-18): "certain Assets may be governed by a separate Provider end user license agreement which shall be provided to you."
