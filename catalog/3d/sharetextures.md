# ShareTextures

Free PBR textures and some 3D models, marketed as CC0. The live license page calls it a **Custom CC0** grant: personal and commercial use, no attribution, modify OK, **no redistribution** on other sites, in plugins, or as collections, and no hotlinking or Blender-addon download proxies. That is not stock CC0. Ship inside a game; do not republish the library.

## Notes

- License: [sharetextures.com/p/license](https://www.sharetextures.com/p/license). Terms: [sharetextures.com/p/terms](https://www.sharetextures.com/p/terms) (dated 10.04.2025)
- Terms also say downloads are CC0 1.0 "However, the license does not waive these Terms of Service". Treat the redistribution and scraping bans as binding
- "CC0 only applies to assets downloaded directly from our site." Do not take a mirror or Discord dump
- Prefer [ambientcg](ambientcg.md) or [cgbookcase](cgbookcase.md) when you need unmodified CC0 without a platform overlay
- Up to 4K. Confirm DirectX vs OpenGL normals per set
