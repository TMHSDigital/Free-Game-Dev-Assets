# ambientCG

1500+ seamless CC0 PBR materials. Standard first stop for environment texturing.

## Notes

- Expect a full stack when the download includes it: albedo/base color, normal (check DirectX vs OpenGL), roughness, metallic, AO, height/displacement
- Not a substitute for calibrated HDRI lighting — pair with [poly-haven](../environment/poly-haven.md)
