# Kenney

Thousands of modular low-poly 3D kits, 2D sheets, UI, and audio packs. Per-pack pages carry a Creative Commons CC0 license field. Consistent grid sizing makes prototyping fast across engines. Preferred over anonymous "CC0" dumps because each pack page states the license.

## Notes

- Homepage copy is "completely free game assets"; CC0 is on the per-pack License field, not the hub
- Paid Kenney tools and the all-in-one itch bundle are separate from the free pack pages
- Space Kit and Industrial Kit are strong for tech/server-room props and flat-shaded pipelines
- 2D Input Prompts (64x64) are catalogued separately for Switch 2 / Quest / Deck coverage
- This hub stays under `3d` as the flagship library; 2D and audio packs have their own entries
