# FreePBR

600+ 2K PBR sets (albedo, normal, roughness, metallic, AO, optional height; Unity metallic/smoothness PSD) by Brian. Homepage markets them as 100% free. The About page is the license: free use in games and 3D **as long as you do not use them commercially**, plus a redistribution ban. Commercial use is a $16 site-wide fee, not a CC0 grant. Prefer [ambientcg](ambientcg.md) when you need a real public-domain stack.

## Notes

- License hub: [freepbr.com/about-free-pbr/](https://freepbr.com/about-free-pbr/). There is no `/license` URL (404 this session)
- Maps are 2048x2048 PNG. Confirm DirectX vs OpenGL normals per zip
- Do not mirror the zips. About asks that source files stay on freepbr.com
- The "Download ALL (600+) ... with Commercial Rights" SKU is the paid grant. Individual $0.00 product pages are the free non-commercial path
- Checklist: license text on About, commercial explicitly false for free files, no CC/NC badge (custom), not a marketplace EULA, named author, not on the high-risk list, catalog claims no rights
