# Avramania Free Collection

203 free low-poly medieval 3D models in one download, taken from nine packs and released as CC0 by the indie studio Avramania. The download comes as glTF (.glb) and FBX. The models were built in Blender with AI tools: 26 of the 203 started as meshes generated by Tripo AI from AI-made concept art, and the rest were made by Blender scripts written with AI coding assistants.

## Notes

- Self-submission: listed by the publisher
- The 26 Tripo-started models were made through Tripo's paid per-use API, then reshaped, cleaned, repainted and reduced by Blender scripts
- The itch.io page carries the "AI Assisted" tag
- This entry covers the free collection only. Each pack also has a paid Premium edition with the rest of its models; those files may not be shared or resold
