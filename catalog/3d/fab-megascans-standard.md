# Fab / Quixel Megascans (Standard License)

Was an entry for Quixel Megascans on Fab as a whole, under the Fab Standard License. Free claiming of the whole Megascans library ended at the end of 2024, and Fab is now mostly a paid store, so this no longer describes a free source. The free Megascans slice is [megascans-starter-content](megascans-starter-content.md); other free Fab listings are [fab-free-content](fab-free-content.md).
