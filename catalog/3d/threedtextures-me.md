# 3DTextures.me

Seamless PBR materials aimed at Blender, Unreal, Unity, and Godot. The F.A.Q. / Textures License page applies CC0 and states the textures no longer belong to the author, including commercial use, with no credit required. The same site also sells paid volume packs. This entry is the CC0 library grant on `/about/`, not a blanket for every SKU on the homepage.

## Notes

- License hub: [3dtextures.me/about/](https://3dtextures.me/about/) titled F.A.Q./Textures License
- Paid "100 Textures Pack" and similar products on the same domain. Re-read that product page before treating a paid zip as CC0
- SBS/SBSAR files are discussed on the same about page. Substance sources follow the same page's CC0 claim only if they are part of the free texture downloads you actually took
- Ko-fi / Patreon are support links, not a second license
- Prefer [ambientcg](ambientcg.md) when you want a larger CC0 catalog with no paid upsell on the same index
