# Charge materials (Blender Studio)

Procedural materials freebie from Blender Studio's *Charge* gallery, by Simon Thommes, shipped as a Blender asset library (`charge_asset_library-materials.zip`, 176.7 MB). The asset's own detail panel lists the licence as **CC-BY**, linked to CC BY 4.0, with credit to Blender Studio. The materials are built for Eevee; bake to PBR textures for other engines.

## Notes

- The licence shows in the asset's detail panel (loaded from `/projects/api/assets/7249/`), not on the gallery card, which is why earlier checks of the card found no badge
- Credit Blender Studio; the panel's tooltip says "you must give attribution to Blender Studio". Naming Simon Thommes as the materials author in the credit line is optional
- Tested with Blender 4.1 per the asset description; install via Preferences > File Paths > Asset Libraries
- Prefer [ambientcg](ambientcg.md) / [poly-haven](../environment/poly-haven.md) when you want CC0 PBR with no credit line
