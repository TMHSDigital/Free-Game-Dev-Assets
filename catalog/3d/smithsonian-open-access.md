# Smithsonian Open Access

Museum 3D scans and media released under CC0 when marked with the Creative Commons Zero icon. High-accuracy heritage assets. Heavy for real-time use; better as reference, cinematics, or decimated in-game props. Not every Smithsonian page is CC0. Filter to CC0-marked items.

## Notes

- Open Access hub: [si.edu/openaccess](https://www.si.edu/openaccess). 3D viewer: [3d.si.edu](https://3d.si.edu)
- Binding CC0 wording is in Smithsonian [Terms of Use](https://www.si.edu/termsofuse), not the marketing banner
