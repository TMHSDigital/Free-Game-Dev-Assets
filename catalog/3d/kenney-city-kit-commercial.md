# Kenney City Kit (Commercial)

50 CC0 modular commercial buildings and skyscrapers (v2.1). Original Kenney work. This is not a GTA V downtown extract and not a FiveM drop-in ydr pack.

## Notes

- Live pack page states Creative Commons CC0 and 50 files. It does not list interchange formats; `formats` matches other Kenney 3D kits in this catalog.
- CC0 lets you re-export to other engines. That path still does not license Rockstar assets. See [`docs/fivem.md`](../../docs/fivem.md).
- Distinct from Kenney's older "Industrial Kit" hub note: this is the City series commercial buildings pack.
