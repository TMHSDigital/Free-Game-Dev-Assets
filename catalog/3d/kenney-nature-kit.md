# Kenney Nature Kit

330 CC0 nature props — trees, rocks, foliage — for outdoor blockouts without paid botanicals.

## Notes

- **Authored at a miniature scale.** Measured 2026-09-23 across all 329 GLBs in the pack: trees have a median height of 1.33 m, rocks 0.32 m. Kenney's own Modular Dungeon walls are 4.2 m and Quaternius trees 7 to 10 m, so this kit needs roughly a 5x uniform scale before it sits beside either. Pick the scale once, at import, not per prop
- **Mixing with [quaternius-stylized-nature-megakit](quaternius-stylized-nature-megakit.md) is a style decision, not a free upgrade.** Beyond the scale gap, the densities differ by about twentyfold: Kenney trees median 166 triangles, Quaternius trees around 3,600. Side by side, one reads as blockout and the other as finished art. Use one per scene, or keep Kenney for distant filler only
- Flat material colours throughout: no textures in any of the 329 models, which is why the files are tiny (7 KB median) and why recolouring is a material edit rather than a texture edit
- Attribution example (optional): `Nature assets by Kenney.nl (CC0)`
