# Effekseer sample effects

User-contributed particle effects for the Effekseer tool. Sample page says they are free to use in games or movies and that these listed effects are CC-0. The Effekseer **tool** is MIT. That is a different grant.

## Notes

- Sample page also says "Almost all effects are distributed under CC-0 License." Treat unlisted or third-party zips as per-file until you see CC-0 on that collection.
- Credit Effekseer and the contributor is appreciated, not required by CC0.
- Runtime: Effekseer plugins exist for Godot, Unity, Unreal, and others. The `.efk` files are not drop-in GPUParticles.
- Do not confuse with Unity Companion particle packs.

- Live Sample Effects page (2026-08-29): "free to use in your games or movies"
