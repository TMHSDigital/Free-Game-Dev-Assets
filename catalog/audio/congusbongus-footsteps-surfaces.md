# Congusbongus Footsteps on Surfaces

Footsteps with many variations per surface: boots on concrete, grass (or dirt and leaves), gravel, metal, polished floor, water and wood, plus a dog, bones and a mech step. CC BY 3.0, and each surface is derived from named Freesound sounds, so the credit line has to name those authors too.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-24:** 78 OGG files in ten folders (`boots`, `grass`, `gravel`, `metal`, `tile`, `water`, `wood`, `dog`, `bones`, `mech`), each with a `license.txt` naming its source sounds. No WAV files, despite what this entry used to say
- **Credit the source authors, not only congusbongus.** Eight folders derive from CC BY 3.0 sounds by swuing, EminYILDIRIM, sabotovat, Eelke, ceberation and Lee Barkovich. `gravel` and `bones` derive from CC0 sounds (Ali_6868, blukotek) and owe nothing extra. The `attribution_string` above covers all of them; trim it if you ship only some surfaces
- The pack also contains macOS `.DS_Store` and `__MACOSX` metadata. Delete it before shipping
- Not CC0. Do not strip attribution
