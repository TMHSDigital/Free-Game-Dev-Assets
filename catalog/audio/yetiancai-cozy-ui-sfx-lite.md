# Cozy UI SFX Free Lite (yetiancai)

Thirty-three short interface sounds for cozy and casual games: clicks, hovers, toggles, confirm and cancel, soft errors, notifications, rewards, level-ups, text blips and menu open/close. The files are 44.1 kHz / 16-bit WAV plus OGG in one 1.3 MB zip, on a name-your-own-price itch page that accepts zero. The page licence allows commercial and non-commercial use in any number of projects, asks for no credit, and forbids reselling or redistributing the raw files. This is the free Lite edition (3 sounds from each of 11 folders) of a paid 190-sound pack; only the free edition belongs in the catalog.

## Notes

- Licence: a short custom licence on the itch page. Commercial use in "unlimited" projects is allowed and credit is "appreciated, not required". The page points to a `LICENSE.txt` inside the zip, which this entry has not read.
- You may not resell or redistribute the raw files, so the sounds cannot go into another sound pack or asset library.
- Provenance: the publisher states the sounds are generated by code written with AI assistance (Grok), and the itch AI disclosure reads "AI Assisted: Code". The audio itself is described as code-generated, not output of an AI audio model, so the entry is not tagged `ai-generated`; read [../../docs/ai-assets.md](../../docs/ai-assets.md) if your store asks about AI tools.
- Upsell: the page links the paid full pack (190 sounds) and other paid packs by the same publisher. They are not covered by this entry.
- Source: the publisher submitted this source in issue #218 and said so. The licence quote was re-read on the live itch page on 2026-11-07.
