# Circuit UI SFX Free Sample

Twelve free retro game sound effects covering UI clicks, confirmations, errors, coin, pickup, powerup, jump, hit impact, laser zap, explosion, success, and secret-discovery cues. The pack is a small standalone CC0 sampler rather than a demo locked behind signup: the dedicated page streams every sound in the browser and offers a direct zip download. WAV files are 44.1 kHz / 16-bit and work in any engine. A larger paid pack exists separately; only the 12 free sample sounds are covered by this entry.

## Notes

- Licence: the 12 sample sounds are CC0; the separate paid Circuit UI SFX Vol. 1 pack is not covered by this entry.
- Attribution: not required. The publisher's optional credit suggestion is "Circuit UI SFX Free Sample by SecondWindMC".
- Provenance: sounds are procedurally generated rather than recorded samples. The publisher used AI assistance in the production workflow; the distributed files are rendered audio, not model-generated recordings.
- Engines: WAV files can be imported directly into Godot, GDevelop, Unity, Unreal, or custom engines. The page links separate Godot and GDevelop usage guides.
- Source: the publisher submitted this entry and operates the linked source page.
