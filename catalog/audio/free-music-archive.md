# Free Music Archive

Tribe of Noise's independent-music archive. Tracks are uploaded under per-file Creative Commons choices. FMA does not give you a site-wide commercial grant. The marketing homepage splits FMA as "Discovery / personal use" versus Tribe of Noise PRO for socials, video, and film. CC-NC tracks are not commercial-game-safe. CC-BY / CC-BY-SA tracks may be, with credit and possible share-alike on the audio derivative. Filter per track like Freesound.

## Notes

- License Guide (2026-08-24): "Most of the music licences you will find on FMA are Creative Commons licences". Artists pick the CC flavor. "FMA cannot license original work to you for commercial, private, or other use"
- That last sentence is aggregator language, not a ban on using a CC-BY file commercially. It means FMA is not the licensor. Open the track
- FAQ for Videos confirms video use when the track's CC conditions match. It does not mention games. Interactive sync plus ND (if any remain) is still the usual trap
- Royalty-free / all-rights-included is the paid PRO product, not the free archive
- Prefer [incompetech](incompetech.md) or [kenney-music-jingles](kenney-music-jingles.md) when you want one license for the whole library
- [freepd](freepd.md) is dead. FMA does not replace it as CC0 music
