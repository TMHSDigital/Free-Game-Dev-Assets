# Kenney Music Jingles

85 short CC0 musical stingers: level complete, game over, item pickup, menu confirm, and similar one-shot cues. This is the catalog's first music source that needs no attribution at all. Every other music entry here is CC-BY or a custom license, so a project that wants zero credit-screen obligations previously had no compliant option for musical cues. These are jingles rather than loops, so they solve stingers and not background scoring.

## Notes

- One-shots of roughly one to four seconds, not loopable beds; pair with a CC-BY track for actual background music if you can carry the credit
- Ships as OGG, which Godot and Unity both stream without conversion
- Released 2014 and unchanged since, so expect a straightforward flat file layout with no per-file metadata
- CC0 means no credit screen entry is required, though Kenney requests a voluntary donation on the download page
- The same download is bundled inside the paid Kenney all-in-one itch bundle; the standalone pack is free and carries the same CC0 terms
