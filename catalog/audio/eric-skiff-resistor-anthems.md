# Eric Skiff, Resistor Anthems

An 18-track album of melodic 8-bit music, CC-BY 4.0, released by the composer "for use in your games, videos, and projects". Against the CC0 [subspaceaudio-5-chiptunes](subspaceaudio-5-chiptunes.md), the trade is a credit line for more than three times the tracks.

## Notes

- **Credit is required, in a set form:** "Music: Eric Skiff - Song Name - Resistor Anthems - Available at http://EricSkiff.com/music", one per track you use. Put it in the in-game credits
- 18 MP3 tracks, downloadable one by one or as a single zip from the page
- Games are named outright in the page's own description, so there is no game-versus-video question here
- Widely used for about a decade, by the author's account "in thousands of videos, games, student projects". The page says nothing about Content ID; check a track in YouTube Studio before relying on it for a trailer
