# Sonniss #GameAudioGDC

Annual professional SFX dumps (multi-GB WAV). Custom EULA grants commercial game use with no attribution. Standalone resale and AI/ML training are prohibited. This is one of the few custom audio licenses that names games and interactive projects in the grant.

## Notes

- Binding text: [gdc-bundle-license](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/)
- Bundle hub: [sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc](https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc/); seasonal portal often at [gdc.sonniss.com](https://gdc.sonniss.com/)
- "May not sell any of the sound effects as they come" still allows shipping them inside a compiled game
- Sonniss also sells small paid impact packs; those are not this free bundle

- Live [GDC bundle license](https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/) (2026-08-24): "personal and commercial projects without attribution to the original creator"
