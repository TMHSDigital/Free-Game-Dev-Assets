# Retro FPS SFX

Retro FPS SFX is a 235-sound royalty-free pack for retro / boomer-shooter style games, with a free 5-sound demo on the itch page and WAV + OGG for every sound. The full pack is paid. Commercial and free games are allowed; do not resell or redistribute the raw files as an asset pack. Credit is not required.
