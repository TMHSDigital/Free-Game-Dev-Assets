# Retro FPS SFX Sampler (Assetsmith)

Eleven free retro first-person-shooter sound effects on their own download page: a pistol shot and reload, a shell casing drop, a small explosion, a concrete impact, a tech door, an ammo pickup, a metal footstep, a monster growl and death, and a menu blip. The files are 44.1 kHz / 16-bit mono WAV plus OGG Vorbis in one zip, with a small Godot 4 demo project. The page licence allows commercial and non-commercial games, allows editing, asks for no credit, and forbids reselling or redistributing the raw files. The sampler is 11 of the 235 sounds in a paid pack; only the free sampler belongs in the catalog.

## Notes

- Licence: a short custom royalty-free licence on the sampler page. No credit required.
- You may not resell or redistribute the raw sound files, so the sounds cannot go into another sound pack or asset library.
- Provenance: the page says ten of the eleven sounds are edits of CC0 Freesound recordings (IDs in `CREDITS.txt` in the zip) and one is synthesized in code, that no AI audio generator was used, and that an AI assistant helped write the build script and the page text. The entry is not tagged `ai-generated`; read [../../docs/ai-assets.md](../../docs/ai-assets.md) if your store asks about AI tools.
- Size: 11 sounds, a little under the dozen suggested in review.
- Upsell: the page links the paid full Retro FPS SFX pack (235 sounds) on itch.io. It is not covered by this entry.
- Source: the publisher submitted this source in PR #315 and said so. The licence quote was re-read on the live sampler page on 2026-11-24.
- Review: read `CREDITS.txt` in the download and confirm the licence matches the page.
