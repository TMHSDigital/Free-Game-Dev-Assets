# 5 Chiptunes (Action), SubspaceAudio

Five looping chiptune tracks by Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio), CC0: Title Screen, Level 1, Level 2, Level 3 and Ending. A complete small soundtrack for a retro platformer or shooter, and the catalog's first chiptune music; until now "chiptune" found only SFX generators.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-23:** five WAV files, 47 MB zipped, plus an `INFO.txt` restating CC0 in the author's words
- The tracks are sampled from a larger paid pack (400 Indie Game Music Loops on itch.io). Only these five are CC0; the paid pack has its own terms
- Widely reused: the page's comments list many jam, itch and store games that use it. That is a mark of quality and also a Content ID consideration; see the note in [the audio README](README.md)
- OpenGameArt is the host; the CC0 statement is both the submission's licence field and the author's own `INFO.txt`, which is stronger than most OGA provenance (see [`docs/provenance.md`](../../docs/provenance.md))
