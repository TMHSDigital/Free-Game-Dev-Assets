# NASA Scientific Visualization Studio

NASA Goddard's visualizations of Earth, the Sun, planets and space missions, as video and stills at up to 8K: data-driven animations of hurricanes, ice, orbits and more.

## Notes

- Some visualizations carry licensed music that is not public domain; use those without the audio track.
- NASA's media guidelines also forbid implying NASA's endorsement in commercial use, and its insignia and logotype are not public domain.
- Source: added for #100 (catalog coverage gaps); the licence was read at the source on 2026-11-18.
