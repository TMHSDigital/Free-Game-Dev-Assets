# Tears of Steel

The Blender Foundation's Mango open movie, a short sci-fi film that mixes live action with CG robots and effects. The project's results are under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, so footage can be reused commercially with credit. It is the live-action option among the Blender open movies, useful for FMV-style cutscenes.

## Notes

- **Credit is owed.** The site's line is "(CC) Blender Foundation | mango.blender.org" unless a file says otherwise
- **Excluded from the licence:** all logos on the site (Blender, Mango, Creative Commons, sponsors) and material not made by the Mango project
- **Games are not named**, but CC-BY grants reuse and distribution in any work; put the credit on a credits screen
- The download page lists the film as 1080p and 4K MOV and WebM files, with MP4 also on offer; check the file before building a pipeline around it
- Live-action footage shows actors; the page says nothing about releases for use outside the film, so keep faces out of anything prominent
