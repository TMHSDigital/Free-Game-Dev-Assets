# Prelinger Archives (Internet Archive)

About 10,000 "ephemeral" films (advertising, educational, industrial and amateur, in the archive's own words) hosted on the Internet Archive. A classic source of period footage for FMV, found-footage horror, in-world TV screens and retro trailers. **Not all of it is public domain**, and the archive says so in capitals.

## Notes

- **Per film, not per collection.** Films that carry the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication can be used "generally without cost or restriction". Films without it may be in copyright, owned by Prelinger, or orphan works. The archive stopped applying the dedication to newer uploads because it "did not work well with many films"
- **How to check a film:** open its Internet Archive page and look for the Creative Commons public-domain licence link. Of the 30 most-downloaded films in the collection on 2026-09-23, 28 carried it and 2 carried none
- A dedicated film can still contain third-party music, images or footage "that might not be free to use outside the context of the original film". Strip the soundtrack if you are unsure of it
- No written clearance from the Internet Archive. Prelinger offers written agreements, and Getty Images licenses higher-quality masters of about 19,000 clips for a fee
- **Credit:** the FAQ answers "Do I need to credit" with "We would be delighted if you credit Prelinger Archives and Internet Archive", which is neither a yes nor a no, so `attribution_required` is `unknown`. Crediting costs nothing
- Each film is offered in several encodings: on the three items checked, MPEG4 at two sizes and h.264, MPEG2, and Ogg video

## Evidence

- Live Prelinger rights FAQ, linked from the collection's rights field (2026-09-23): "DO NOT ASSUME that all Prelinger Archives films are in the public domain, or that all of them carry Creative Commons licenses."
- Same page (2026-09-23): "Many files are available under the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication, which means you can use them according to the terms of the license, generally without cost or restriction."
- Same page (2026-09-23): "public domain materials may occasionally incorporate copyrighted elements (such as music, images and archival footage) that might not be free to use outside the context of the original film."
- Internet Archive search API, `collection:prelinger` sorted by downloads (2026-09-23): 10,468 items; 28 of the top 30 carry `licenseurl` `http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/`
- Collection description (2026-09-23): "a collection of over 60,000 \"ephemeral\" (advertising, educational, industrial, and amateur) films"
