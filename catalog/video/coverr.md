# Coverr

Free stock video, plus some music. The Coverr licence is an irrevocable, worldwide copyright licence to use and modify clips for free, commercially, with no credit. It also bans using clips to train AI, not only scraping them.

## Notes

- **Use in a game:** games are not named. The grant is general ("download, copy, modify, perform, and use ... including for commercial purposes"), and its three exclusions (a competing service, AI training, using the brands shown) do not cover shipping a clip inside a game
- **No AI training:** clips "must not be used to train AI algorithms, develop AI models, or serve as part of any dataset". Relevant if your pipeline feeds footage to a model
- **Releases are not passed on.** Coverr says it obtains model releases but does not provide them, and holds none for brands, trademarks or landmarks in shot
- The site also sells a Coverr+ subscription and AI tools; the free licence applies to free downloads, and says attribution is not required for either

## Evidence

- Live [License](https://coverr.co/license) (2026-09-23): "Coverr.co grants you an irrevocable, non-exclusive, worldwide copyright license to download, copy, modify, perform, and use videos and music from Coverr.co for free, including for commercial purposes"
- Same page (2026-09-23): "You are not required to add an attribution credit to the original creator or to Coverr.co" and "Our videos and music must not be used to train AI algorithms, develop AI models, or serve as part of any dataset for such purposes."
