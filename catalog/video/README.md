# Video: stock footage and archival clips

Clips for menu backgrounds, in-world screens, FMV, cutscenes and trailer B-roll. Not 3D
mocap (`animation/`) and not sprite sheets (`2d/`).

## Choosing a video source

Licences were read at each source on 2026-09-23. **None of these names video games.**
The first three grant use in general terms with a short list of exclusions, none of which
covers shipping a clip inside a game; see
[`docs/game-vs-video-licensing.md`](../../docs/game-vs-video-licensing.md) for why the
wording matters.

| If you need | Take | Credit | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| General stock footage | [pexels-videos](pexels-videos.md) | None | No reselling unaltered clips or redistributing them on other stock sites; no scraping |
| The same, a second pool | [pixabay-videos](pixabay-videos.md) | None | No standalone redistribution: fine inside a game, not as a clip pack |
| Clips with an explicit irrevocable grant | [coverr](coverr.md) | None | No AI training; no releases passed on for people or brands in shot |
| Envato's free clips | [mixkit-stock-video](mixkit-stock-video.md) | None | Games are left off the licence's use list, though Mixkit's SFX licence names them. `needs-review` |
| Period footage, films from the archive | [prelinger-archives](prelinger-archives.md) | Unclear | Per film: only films carrying the Creative Commons public-domain dedication are free to use |
| Government archival shots, searchable by shot | [destockd](destockd.md) | None | Aggregator; FedFlix is usually U.S. public domain but is not certified per clip |

**What is in the shot is a separate question.** These licences cover the footage, not the
faces, logos and buildings in it. Pixabay and Coverr put those rights on you in so many
words, and Coverr and Destockd say they provide no releases. Keep recognisable
people and brands out of anything prominent, such as a title screen.
