# Pexels Videos

Large free stock video library from Pexels, a Canva brand. The Pexels License lets you use and modify clips for free with no credit, and names apps among its use cases. Good for menu backgrounds, in-world TV screens, trailer B-roll and loading-screen loops.

## Notes

- **Use in a game:** the licence does not say "games", but it is a general grant ("free to use", "You can modify") and lists "On your website, blog or app" as a use case. None of its prohibitions covers shipping a clip inside software
- **What you may not do:** sell unaltered copies, redistribute clips "on other stock photo or wallpaper platforms", show identifiable people "in a bad light", imply that people or brands in a clip endorse your product, or use a clip as part of a trademark
- The licence covers the footage, not what is in it. Recognisable people, logos and buildings can carry their own rights, and the licence says nothing about model or property releases
- Contributors upload the clips; Pexels hosts them. For anything prominent (a face on your title screen), check who shot it
- The Terms of Service ban bulk or systematic copying and scraping, including for machine learning. Download clip by clip, or use the official API
