# Quaternius Cube World Kit

Voxel-style cube characters, animals, enemies, and environment models in one kit (108 models). This is the catalog's voxel *character* coverage; MagicaVoxel remains the editor. Textured. FBX / OBJ / glTF / Blend.

## Notes

- This is a mixed kit. You also get terrain/prop cubes. Catalogued under characters because that is the unmet need, not because the zip is characters-only.
- Animations are on the cube rigs. Not a Mixamo retarget source.
- Standard vs Source split is the usual Quaternius pattern. License badge on this pack page is CC0.
