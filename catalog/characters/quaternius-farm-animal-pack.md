# Quaternius Farm Animal Pack

Seven farm animals, each with its own animation set. This is the catalog's actual quadruped coverage. Mixamo will not retarget onto these; the clips live on the animal rigs. Untextured. FBX / OBJ / Blend.

## Notes

- Pack page lists no glTF. Import FBX and keep the included animations.
- Standard download is the free kit. Source Blend extras are a Patreon/paid SKU, still labeled CC0 on other Quaternius packs. Confirm the badge on this page if the SKU mix changes.
- Not a humanoid retarget library. Do not expect Mixamo or UAL clips to land cleanly.
