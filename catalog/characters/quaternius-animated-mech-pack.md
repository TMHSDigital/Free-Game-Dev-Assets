# Quaternius Animated Mech Pack

Four mechs, each with its own animation set. Non-humanoid sci-fi bodies. Complements robot enemies in Sci-Fi Essentials when you need a vehicle-scale mech rather than a biped drone. Textured.

## Notes

- Clips are on the mech rigs. UAL will not drop on these.
- Pack page lists FBX / OBJ / Blend; formats badge also shows glTF. Prefer glTF if your importer likes it; fall back to FBX.
- Small pack (4 models). Not a modular mech kit.
