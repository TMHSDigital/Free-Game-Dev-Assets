# CSAF PSX Characters Sampler

17 free low poly PS1 style 3D characters from the studio Core Systems Asset Factory (CSAF), one taken from each of its paid PSX character packs, from a knight and a plague doctor to a patrol officer and a shambler. The page lists 52 skins across the 17, about 1,500 triangles per character, 256 px PNG textures and one shared humanoid skeleton with 45 animation clips and a T pose, delivered as GLB, FBX, OBJ and the Blender source, with a PSX shader for Godot 4. The page says the bodies are built in code and the textures painted by AI image models over the models' own renders; the skeleton and clips are the CC0 Quaternius Universal Animation Library and the bodies come from MakeHuman.

## Notes

- Self-submission: listed by the publisher
- Made with AI: the page states it in its own words (quoted in Evidence), and the itch.io AI disclosure on the page reads "AI Assisted, Code, Graphics"
- Engines: the page says the GLB files were tested in a blank Godot 4.7.2 project under Forward+, Compatibility and Mobile, and that Unity 6 and Unreal 5 "were not run here"
- This entry covers the free sampler only. The publisher sells the full casts as paid packs and as one paid PSX Character Library on the same itch.io account; those are not covered here
- Review: a maintainer reading of the publisher's own terms (commercial use, no credit, no resale or redistribution of the models as assets) on the live page.
