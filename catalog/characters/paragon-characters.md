# Paragon Characters (Epic, on Fab)

One entry for the free character packs Epic released from its cancelled MOBA Paragon, now listed on Fab under the Epic Games seller. A Fab search for "paragon" filtered to Epic Games returned 24 listings on 2026-10-13, among them Greystone, Sparrow, Wraith, Gideon, Wukong, Kwang, Morigesh, Yin, Terra, Serath, The Fey, Sevarog, Rampage, Aurora, Khaimera, Zinx, Grux, Countess, Muriel, Phase, Steel, Shinbi, Revenant and a Minions pack. Each pack includes the character model, animations, Animation Blueprints, skins and FX as Unreal Engine assets. They are AAA-quality heroes, heavy for small projects, and they come as Unreal content rather than standalone FBX.

## Notes

- Each listing says the content is "free to use as you like in your Unreal Engine projects" and carries the Fab "Standard License" in its Details panel. None of the three listings read (Greystone, Sparrow, Wraith) shows a "UE-Only Content" line, unlike other Epic samples such as [Electric Dreams Env](../environment/electric-dreams-environment.md). Whether use outside Unreal is permitted is therefore not settled: the description wording points at Unreal projects, the Standard License summary says use "is not limited to Unreal Engine". `engine_lock` is left out and the entry stays `needs-review` until a clearer Epic statement is found
- Trademark condition on every listing read: "You may not use the trademark PARAGON to advertise or name your game."
- Greystone and Sparrow were "created based on face scans from https://home.otoy.com"; the listings attach no extra condition to that
- No redistribution: the Fab EULA summary says you may not "Resell or redistribute the asset for free on a standalone basis or allow others to do the same". Do not commit the packs in source form to a public repository
- `ue_versions` comes from the Greystone and Sparrow listings ("4.19 – 4.27 and 5.0 – 5.8"); the Wraith page did not show a version range when read. Check each pack's own listing before downloading
- The entry `url` is a Fab search ("paragon", seller Epic Games) because the packs have no shared listing page. If Fab changes its search parameters, open the Epic Games seller page (`fab.com/sellers/Epic Games`, checked 2026-10-22) and search for Paragon there
