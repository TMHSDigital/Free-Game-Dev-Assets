# Kenney Animated Characters

Kenney's "Animated Characters" series: three small packs of rigged, animated low-poly humanoids with swappable PNG skins. [Protagonists](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-protagonists) (2020; skater, cyborg, criminal), [Retro](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-retro) (2022) and [Survivors](https://kenney.nl/assets/animated-characters-survivors) (2019; zombie and survivor). All three are CC0.

## Notes

- One entry for the series, since each pack is the same rig and format with different skins
- Contents read from each pack's download ZIP (2026-09-26): 4 FBX files plus PNG skins in each, and SVG files in Protagonists and Survivors
- The bundled `License.txt` says credit is welcome but "not a requirement"
- Attribution example (optional): `Characters by Kenney.nl (CC0)`
