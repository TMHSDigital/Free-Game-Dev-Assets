# Quaternius Animated Fish Pack

Seven aquatic species, each with its own animations. Non-quadruped creature motion. Untextured. FBX / OBJ / Blend. No glTF on the pack page.

## Notes

- Swim cycles are on these rigs. There is no fish retarget library in this catalog.
- Untextured: assign a simple gradient or flat color, or bake your own.
- Tiny pack. Use it for water fauna, not as a general creature solution.
