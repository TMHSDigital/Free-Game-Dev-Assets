# Pencil2D

Small, simple frame-by-frame animation program with raster and vector layers, for hand-drawn sprite animation and animatics. Runs on Windows, macOS, Linux and FreeBSD.

## Notes

- **GPL version 2 only:** the source headers grant "version 2 of the License" with no "or any later version" clause, so the licence is `GPL-2.0-only`
- The GPL covers the program. Animations you draw are your own; the homepage says it is free to use "even commercially"
- Filed in `tools/` with the other general 2D art and animation editors
