# AccuRig

Free stand-alone auto-rigger for bipeds (and some creature proportions). Export FBX/USD for Unity, Unreal, Blender, etc. Best when the **mesh is 100% your own**.

## Notes

- Terms: Reallusion's own AccuRIG FAQ says the tool is free for personal and commercial use and points to the Reallusion Software EULA (dated May 25th, 2026), which covers all Reallusion products. The EULA has no credit or attribution clause for your project; it only says Reallusion logos and trademarks need approval
- The EULA lets you sell or distribute models and animation assets "only when embedded within games, applications, interactive projects, or in 3D print format". Rigging a mesh does not change that mesh's own licence: the FAQ says "Any model that is rigged with AccuRIG should follow its own EULA"
- The sample characters bundled in AccuRIG and ActorCore motions/content fall under the separate Reallusion Content EULA. Do not assume free AccuRig implies free ActorCore packs
- Windows only, per the same FAQ
