# OpenToonz

Full production 2D animation suite, open-sourced by DWANGO from the Toonz software used in studio animation work. Covers drawing, scanning and cleanup, timing (Xsheet), effects and compositing. Heavier than [pencil2d](pencil2d.md), aimed at longer or more polished animation.

## Notes

- The site says OpenToonz can be used free of charge for both commercial and non-commercial projects, and the source is New BSD
- The site points to separate "rules of use" for the installer build, but the page links no such document; it was not read for this entry. The commercial statement above is on the project's own homepage
- Third-party code in `thirdparty/` and the bundled MyPaint brushes carry their own licences, per the `LICENSE.txt` header
- Filed in `tools/` with the other general 2D art and animation editors
