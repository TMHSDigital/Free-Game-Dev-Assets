# Pixel Art Upscaler (PixelArtHive)

Free, no-account browser tool that scales pixel art up or down without blur. Nearest neighbour is the default, at whole-number scales from 2× to 32× or down to fractions such as ½× and ⅛×. It can also fit a sprite to an exact canvas size with padding, rebuild the native pixel grid of an already enlarged or JPG-compressed sprite, and apply edge-smoothing scalers such as Scale2x, Eagle, MMPX, ScaleFX and xBR. Input is PNG, GIF (animated too), JPG, WebP or BMP; output is PNG for stills, GIF for animations, or an Aseprite file. Images are processed in the browser, and the site states it uses fixed algorithms, not generative AI.
