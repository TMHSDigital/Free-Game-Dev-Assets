# Gaea Community Edition

QuadSpinner terrain tool with a free download on the product site. The previous catalog line called Community Edition non-commercial from research notes, not from a quoted EULA. This session the legal page points at a PDF (`Gaea2-2025-07.pdf`, "July 14, 2025 (Active)") that was not extracted as text.
