# Ardour

A full digital audio workstation for recording, editing and mixing on Linux, macOS and Windows. The source is free; the project asks for payment for its ready-made binaries.

## Notes

- The GPL covers the program. Audio you record and export with it is yours.
- Official binaries are pay-what-you-want with a free demo build; building from source is free.
- Source: added for #100 (catalog coverage gaps); the licence was read at the source on 2026-11-18.
