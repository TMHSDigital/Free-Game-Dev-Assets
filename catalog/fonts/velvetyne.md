# Velvetyne

French libre type foundry (VTF). Display and experimental faces, not a UI workhorse set. About page: all hosted fonts are libre; you may use, modify, and redistribute, including commercial work, and you must credit the designer and the foundry. Lineal's typeface page names SIL Open Font License 1.1. About also says they are still evolving licensing and OFL is a "fair foundation" that does not entirely meet their needs. Check the OFL.txt in the font you actually download.

## Notes

- Credit is required by the foundry's about page even when a face is OFL (OFL itself does not always demand a credits-screen line). Use `attribution_string`
- Modified derivatives must ship under the same licence as the original face
- Do not treat this as a substitute for Inter / Noto in HUD body type. These are display/experimental
- Contrast [fontshare](fontshare.md): Velvetyne states OFL plus libre freedoms in HTML. Fontshare's ITF terms were not readable this catalog
