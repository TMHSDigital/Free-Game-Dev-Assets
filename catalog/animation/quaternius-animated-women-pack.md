# Quaternius Animated Women Pack

Four rigged female low-poly human models with many animations, in FBX, OBJ and Blend. Small, quick to drop into a prototype or to harvest clips from. Models are textured and each has its own rig.

## Notes

- Page lists 4 animated, textured models (February 2019)
- Companion to the Animated Men pack
