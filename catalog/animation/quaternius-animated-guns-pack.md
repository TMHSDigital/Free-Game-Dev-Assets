# Quaternius Animated Guns Pack

Six animated low-poly guns (P90, revolver, pistol, shotgun, sniper rifle and similar) in FBX, OBJ and Blend. Handy for FPS or shooter prototypes needing fire and reload motion on the weapon itself. Weapon-only animation, no arms or character.

## Notes

- Page lists 6 animated, textured models (June 2018)
- Pair with a first-person arms rig of your own
- Attribution example (optional): `Animations by Quaternius (CC0)`
- Quaternius published a separate Quaternius Asset License (QAL v1.0, dated 2026-08-28) that says it governs future releases and that earlier versions are not changed retroactively. This 2018-2019 pack page still shows CC0. Keep a copy of the pack page or download date with your project files.
