# KayKit Character Animations

CC0 humanoid clips for KayKit characters (also retargetable onto other humanoids). Split by Rig_Medium and Rig_Large. Mixamo alternative for stylized kits. 161 animations across general, movement, melee, ranged, sim, special, and tool sets.

## Notes

- FBX and glTF; retarget in-engine onto non-KayKit humanoids
- Special skeleton variants are for the Skeleton pack, not the default adventurers
- Author ask on other KayKit pages: do not resell unmodified copies
