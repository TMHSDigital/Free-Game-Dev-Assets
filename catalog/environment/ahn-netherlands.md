# Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland (AHN)

Dutch national height map built from airborne lidar, with raster grids (0.5 m and 5 m) and point clouds, downloadable per map sheet through PDOK. Excellent for flat polder and urban terrain. Marked needs-review: the AHN page says the data is free to use "without restrictions" but names no licence instrument this session, so the exact licence is unconfirmed.

## Notes

- AHN1, AHN2 and AHN3 are the open datasets named on the page. Newer releases (AHN4 and later) were not confirmed on that page this session.
- Subunits are the map sheets for the 0.5 m raster and the point clouds; units are used for the 5 m raster. Sheet layout files are on the page.
- Format names (GeoTIFF raster, LAZ point cloud) are from general knowledge of the PDOK downloads, not from the page; confirm on download.
- Before moving to active, find a licence statement (for example a CC0 or public domain mark) on a PDOK or Nationaal Georegister record and quote it.
- The licence page https://www.ahn.nl/licentie returned 404 this session.

## Evidence

- AHN open data page (2026-10-04), in Dutch: "De datasets van het AHN1, AHN2 en AHN3 zijn beschikbaar als Open Data."
- Same page (2026-10-04): "door iedereen gratis en zonder beperkingen te gebruiken is" (free for anyone to use without restrictions).
- No licence name located on the page; status is needs-review for that reason.
