# Ninja Adventure

Pixel-Boy + AAA CC0 16×16 top-down ninja town pack: 50+ characters, 30+ monsters, bosses, tiles (incl. floor autotile), UI, VFX, 100+ SFX, 37 music tracks, fonts, WIP Godot example.

## Notes

- 3/4 camera — don’t composite with pure top-down or side-scroller sheets
- **Four-directional, confirmed.** Character sheets are 64x112 PNGs on a 16px grid: four columns for down, up and both sides, seven rows of frames covering walk, attack, item and death. Checked 2026-09-22 against `content/character/*/sprite.png` in the author's own game repository, [pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure](https://github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure), which is built from this art. The itch pack is the larger release; the per-character layout is the same
- Directional siblings in this catalog: [armm1998-zelda-like](armm1998-zelda-like.md) also turns; [kenney-roguelike-characters](kenney-roguelike-characters.md) does not
