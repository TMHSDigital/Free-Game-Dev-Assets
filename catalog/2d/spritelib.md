# SpriteLib

Ari Feldman's classic 1996 sprite collection, re-released by WidgetWorx: 44 PNG sheets of static and animated sprites for breakout/paddle, maze, platform, shooter and a cartoon "Fish Dish" game, plus grids. Distributed under the Common Public License 1.0, a software licence applied here to art.

## Notes

- The zip is named `spritelib_gpl.zip`, but its bundled `license.rtf` says Common Public License 1.0, not GPL
- CPL 1.0 allows commercial use and redistribution; it asks that copyright notices be kept and that the licence accompany source-form distributions. No credits-screen line is required
- Old art (1996-era) by the author's own account
