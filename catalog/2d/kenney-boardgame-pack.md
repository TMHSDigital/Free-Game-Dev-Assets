# Kenney Board Game Pack

CC0 board-game kit: playing cards and backs, poker chips, dice, and pieces in seven colours, with a dozen matching sounds. From 2014, and not pixel art: it ships SVG sources alongside the PNGs.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-23:** 539 PNGs in `Cards`, `Chips`, `Dice` and `Pieces` (Black, Blue, Green, Purple, Red, White, Yellow), SVG sources in `Vector`, XML sprite sheets
- **Includes sound:** 12 OGG files in `Bonus`, for placing and sliding cards, chips colliding, and dice being shuffled and thrown. Also CC0
- Also contains `.swf` files from the Flash era. Ignore them
- For a pixel-art deck, see [kenney-playing-cards-pack](kenney-playing-cards-pack.md)
