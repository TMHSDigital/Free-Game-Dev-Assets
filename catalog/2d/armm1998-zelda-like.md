# Zelda-like tilesets and sprites (ArMM1998)

CC0 16x16 overworld, cave and indoor tilesets with a sword-swinging character, effects, objects and UI, by ArMM1998 on OpenGameArt. A complete small 3/4 RPG kit from a single author, which makes it the non-Kenney alternative when you do not want the whole catalog in one house style.

## Notes

- **Four-directional, confirmed.** Opened `gfx/character.png` from the pack's own `gfx_3.zip` on 2026-09-22: a 272x256 sheet of 16x32 frames carrying down, up and both side facings, with a four-frame walk and a sword-attack set in each direction. The source description says only "Character with sword animation and character tamplet"; the sheet is the answer
- OpenGameArt is a host, not a rights holder. The CC0 here is the submitter’s declaration; see [`docs/provenance.md`](../../docs/provenance.md) before shipping, as with any aggregator upload
- Single-author kit, so the art is consistent but the volume is small next to [kenney-roguelike-rpg-pack](kenney-roguelike-rpg-pack.md)
- 3/4 view in the Zelda mould, matching [ninja-adventure](ninja-adventure.md) rather than the orthographic packs
