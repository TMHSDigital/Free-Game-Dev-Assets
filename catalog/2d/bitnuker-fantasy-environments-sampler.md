# BitNuker Fantasy Environments Sampler

Free 19-file pixel art environment sampler for fantasy games: four seamless ground tiles (fairy meadow, glowshroom floor, rune stone, lava crust), a seamless magic water tile, three runestone path pieces, an enchanted cliff piece, three transparent overlay decals (rune circle, fairy ring, magic sparkles), two full-frame 1920x1080 skies (enchanted sunset, aurora night), four transparent sky elements (giant moon, dragon silhouette, floating isle, ringed moon), and a transparent castle skyline horizon strip. Each PNG embeds its title, inventory and usage notes in the file metadata. Made with generative AI assistance (disclosed on the listing). License: commercial and non-commercial game use, modify freely, no resale or redistribution of the raw files.
