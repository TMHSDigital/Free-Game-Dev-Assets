# SunnyLand (ansimuz)

Free all-in-one 16-bit platformer kit: animated player and enemies, items, props, VFX, and music. This is the pack in the ansimuz store whose itch Asset license field is explicitly CC0. ansimuz also sells paid collections (Gothicvania, Warped, TinyRPG, The 16-bit Collection). Those are not covered by this entry.

## Notes

- itch metadata (2026-08-24): Asset license Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal. No generative AI
- The same listing links a Unity Asset Store preview. Unity/Fab terms are a different EULA. Use the itch download if you want the CC0 field we quoted
- Sibling free pages on the same account are not automatically CC0. SunnyLand Tall Forest did not expose an Asset license field in this session
- The Ansimuz Legacy Collection page has a comment from the author saying commercial use is OK, but it did not show a CC0 Asset license field this session. Do not inherit SunnyLand's CC0 onto that zip
- Music in this pack is under the same itch CC0 field as the sprites, which is the catalog's first full-length CC0 music sitting inside a game kit rather than as one-shot jingles

## Evidence
