# GameArt2D freebies

HD side-scroller characters, tilesets, backgrounds, and GUI on the Freebies page. License page splits two grants: Freebies are CC0 / public domain (games named). Paid assets are a different license with a redistribution ban. This entry is only the Freebies section.

## Notes

- License hub: [gameart2d.com/license.html](https://www.gameart2d.com/license.html)
- Free Assets License: commercial or non-commercial including games; credit optional.
- Paid Assets License: commercial games OK, but no reselling/sharing the assets themselves. Do not copy that grant onto freebies, and do not copy CC0 onto paid SKUs.
- Mix of platformer sheets and GUI. `camera_perspective` flags the character/tileset majority; UI packs are 2d_flat chrome.
- Not featured. Prefer Kenney / ansimuz CC0 kits when you want a shorter provenance story.
