# Pixel Frog

Modular pixel packs for 2D platformers and tiny RTS-style games (Pixel Adventure, Treasure Hunters, Kings and Pigs, Tiny Swords). Useful as complete character-plus-tiles kits rather than Kenney-style tile-only starters. **Do not treat the whole store as CC0.** Pixel Adventure, Treasure Hunters, and Kings and Pigs are CC0 on their itch pages. Tiny Swords is a custom commercial-ok grant with a redistribution ban, and only an older zip is labeled CC0.

## Notes

- Pixel Adventure is PNG only; animations are 20 FPS / 50 ms per frame on the pack page
- Tiny Swords splits a Free Pack (name-your-price) from a paid Enemy Pack at $9.75. The current Free Pack zip is not the file named `TS_old version_CC0 Licensed`
- Tiny Swords custom terms: personal and commercial use, modify OK, credit optional, **no redistribute / resell / repackage** even of modified files. That is stricter than CC0
- itch Asset license field is CC0 on the three packs named above. Tiny Swords has no Asset license field; the grant is the on-page bullet list
- Unity store mirrors, if any, are a different EULA. This entry is the itch publisher
