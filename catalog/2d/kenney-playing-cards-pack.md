# Kenney Playing Cards Pack

A full deck of pixel-art playing cards in three sizes, CC0, from 2020. Enough for solitaire, poker, blackjack or any card game with a standard deck.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-23:** 285 PNGs in `Cards (large)`, `Cards (medium)` and `Cards (small)`, named by suit and rank (`card_clubs_02.png`), plus card backs and a tile sheet with CSV data
- Pixel art. For non-pixel cards plus chips and dice, [kenney-boardgame-pack](kenney-boardgame-pack.md) is the older set, with SVG sources
- Card faces only: no table, felt or chips here
