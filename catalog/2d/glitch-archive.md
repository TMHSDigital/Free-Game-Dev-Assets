# Glitch archive

10k+ drawings, sprites, and backgrounds from the shuttered MMO Glitch. Tiny Speck dedicated the art to the public domain. The OpenGameArt post is titled as a CC0 release. Prefer community PNG/SVG conversions; a large slice of the original dump is Adobe FLA.

## Notes

- This OGA page is a news post quoting Tiny Speck, not a per-file OGA license widget
- Tiny Speck wording is a public-domain dedication plus copyright relinquishment; OGA framed that as CC0 in the title
- Original announcement URL cited on the post: glitchthegame.com/public-domain-game-art/ (not re-fetched this session)
- FLA packages need Flash-era tools or community rips; do not treat FLA as engine-ready

## Evidence

- Live OpenGameArt post (2026-08-24), quoting Tiny Speck: "dedicated to the public domain"
- Same quoted announcement (2026-08-24): "used by anyone, for any purpose"
