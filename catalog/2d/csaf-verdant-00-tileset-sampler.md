# CSAF Verdant 00 Tileset Sampler

A free sampler of the Verdant pixel art tileset series from the studio Core Systems Asset Factory (CSAF): 878 tiles at 16x16, one complete 47 mask autotile set and its ground tiles from each of 16 paid packs, from flagstone dungeons and castle walls to deep ocean coral, desert oases and an orbital station. The page lists 752 autotile tiles and 126 fill tiles across 31 materials, a 64x40 catalogue map as PNG, JSON and a Tiled .tmx, 126 4x4 proof renders, and a tiles.json giving each tile's role, material, passability and the paid pack it comes from. Importers for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot ship in the download. The page says every tile is drawn by a procedural generator whose code was written with AI assistance, with no diffusion model in the tile pipeline.

## Notes

- Self submission: listed by the publisher
- Made with AI: the page states it in its own words (quoted in Evidence), and the itch.io AI disclosure on the page reads "AI Assisted, Code, Graphics"
- Engines: the page says importers for RPG Maker MZ, Tiled and Godot ship in the download, "and each was run by the engine it targets"
- This entry covers the free sampler only. The publisher sells the full tilesets as paid packs and as one paid Verdant Library on the same itch.io account; those are not covered here
- Review: a maintainer reading of the publisher's own terms (commercial use, no credit, no resale or redistribution of the art itself) on the live page.
