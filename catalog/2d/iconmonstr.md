# iconmonstr

Large single-author set of simple monochrome icons by Alexander Kahlkopf, each in fill and outline versions on a 24-pixel grid, downloadable one at a time as SVG or PNG. The licence is the publisher's own agreement, not an open licence: commercial use in products and applications without credit is granted, but the icons may not be redistributed or resold as icons, and they may not be the main component of what you ship.

## Notes

- Grant: commercial and non-commercial projects, services and products, no attribution; you may adapt or recolour the icons
- Forbidden: selling, redistributing, sublicensing or sharing the icons themselves; using one as part of a logo, trademark or service mark; pornographic, defamatory, racist or religiously offensive use
- The icons must serve "as part of the design," not as "the basis or main component" of the product, so HUD and menu glyphs in a game fit; an icon-pack product or an icon-picker app built from them does not
- The licence is "single-user" and non-transferable. Each person who downloads is the licensee, so a team member passing the files to a contractor outside the game build is not covered
- Section 4: some icons depict third-party logos or brands, and the licence leaves those rights to you. Skip brand icons for a commercial game
- The Q&A says bulk-downloading the whole set "violates the license agreement"; take the icons you need
- Games are not named, but "applications" and "products" distributed by you are, and icons are not the music/SFX case where the catalog needs games named
