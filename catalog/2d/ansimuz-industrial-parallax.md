# Industrial Parallax Background (ansimuz)

A night-time industrial skyline split into four seamless, looping layers for a scrolling side-view game, by Luis Zuno (ansimuz), CC0. The catalog's first background supplied as separate layers: move each at its own speed and you have parallax.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-23:** four PNG layers (`foreground` 272x104, `buildings` 272x150, `far-buildings` 213x142, `bg` 272x160), the layered PSD, and a `license.txt`
- Small pixel-art canvas, about 272 pixels wide. Scale with nearest-neighbour filtering (in Godot, the texture filter set to Nearest) or it will blur
- One scene. ansimuz is the author of [ansimuz-sunnyland](ansimuz-sunnyland.md); ansimuz's paid packs on itch.io are separate and not covered here
- OpenGameArt hosts it; the CC0 is in both the submission's licence field and the author's bundled `license.txt`
