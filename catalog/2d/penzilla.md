# Penzilla

Penny / Penzilla Design: mixed free and paid pixel interiors, GUI, icons, and character sheets on itch. Hub copy says royalty-free game assets. Free listings this session (Giant Basic GUI Bundle, Hooded Protagonist, Top-Down Retro Interior) all say "Royalty free and commercial use" and attach `PenzillaDesign_StandardLicense.pdf`. That PDF was not readable without an itch download, and none of those pages expose an itch Asset license field. Do not treat this store as CC0.

## Notes

- Paid packs sit in the same account (Colorful Dungeon, food icons, master collection). Free-page terms do not automatically cover paid SKUs
- "Once purchased (see also licence)" appears even on name-your-price / free listings. Read the PDF in the zip before shipping
- Hooded Protagonist is a 32x32 pixel character with eight animations. Interior pack is top-down furniture, not a full outdoor tileset
- itch marketplace EULA is extra. This entry is the publisher's own Standard License, which we could not quote
