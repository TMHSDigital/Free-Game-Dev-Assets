# Penzilla

Penny / Penzilla Design: pixel interiors, GUI, icons, and character sheets on itch, some free (name your own price) and some paid. **Free downloads are for non-commercial use only.** Every pack ships `PenzillaDesign_StandardLicense.pdf`, which grants commercial use only "provided a valid, fully paid invoice or proof of purchase is available", one project per purchase, and the publisher confirms on the pack pages that commercial use means paying the suggested price. The "Royalty free and commercial use" line on each page describes the paid licence, not the $0 download. Do not treat this store as CC0.

## Notes

- Commercial use: pay the suggested price on the pack page; each purchase covers ONE project, and further commercial projects need another purchase
- Credit is mandatory, commercial or not, in any public project: "Graphics created by Penzilla Design", visible where other contributors are credited. If you edit the art, list your edits next to the credit
- Public projects must also send a link to PenzillaDesign@gmail.com (Standard Licence, "PUBLIC PROJECTS")
- No resale or redistribution of the files, no use in other asset packs or templates, no logos or trademarks, no AI/ML training without permission
- The PDF read on 2026-10-04 came from Hooded Protagonist ("Updated Dec. 2024"); the other packs attach a file of the same name, but check the copy in your download
- Hooded Protagonist is a 32x32 pixel character with eight animations. Interior pack is top-down furniture, not a full outdoor tileset
