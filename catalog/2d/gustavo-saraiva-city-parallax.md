# City Parallax Pixel Art (Gustavo Saraiva)

A tiny pixel-art city background in four parallax layers at 240x135, CC0. 240x135 is exactly an eighth of 1920x1080, so it scales cleanly to full HD at 8x.

## Notes

- **Measured from the download, 2026-09-23:** four PNGs at 240x135 each: `BG`, `Background 1`, `Middle`, `Foreground`. The whole archive is 6 KB
- Low detail by design. Suits a low-resolution pixel game; at higher resolutions it will read as sparse
- Scale with nearest-neighbour filtering to keep the pixels sharp
