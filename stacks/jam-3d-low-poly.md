# Game jam: 3D low-poly

For a jam entry in Godot, Unity, Unreal or anything that reads glTF or FBX. The picks share a flat, low-poly look, the character is already rigged and animated, and there is a greybox set for the first hours, before the art goes in. Every pick is a direct download with no account, and none asks for credit.

## Art

- **First-hours blockout:** [KayKit Prototype Bits](../catalog/3d/kaykit-prototype-bits.md). 64 greybox meshes on one gradient atlas, including a character that takes KayKit's animations.
- **Level kit:** [KayKit Dungeon Pack](../catalog/3d/kaykit-dungeon-pack.md). Over 200 modular walls, floors and props that snap together into rooms.
- **Outdoors:** [Kenney Nature Kit](../catalog/3d/kenney-nature-kit.md). 330 trees, rocks and plants for outdoor levels.
- **Player and enemies:** [KayKit Adventurers Character Pack](../catalog/characters/kaykit-adventurers.md). Rigged and animated low-poly adventurers with more than 25 weapons and accessories.
- **More animation:** [Quaternius Universal Animation Library](../catalog/animation/quaternius-universal-animation-library.md). Over 120 humanoid clips on one retarget-friendly rig, with export sets tested in Godot, Unity and Unreal.
- **Sky and lighting:** [Poly Haven](../catalog/environment/poly-haven.md). HDRI skies for image-based lighting, up to 8K.
- **Menus and HUD:** [Kenney UI Pack](../catalog/2d/kenney-ui-pack.md). 430 buttons, panels and sliders.

## Audio

- **Music:** [Abstraction Music Loop Bundle](../catalog/audio/tallbeard-abstraction-music-loop-bundle.md). Over 200 loops, so every level can have its own.
- **Footsteps and weapons:** [Kenney RPG Audio](../catalog/audio/kenney-rpg-audio.md). 50 footstep, weapon and foley clips.
- **Hits:** [Kenney Impact Sounds](../catalog/audio/kenney-impact-sounds.md). 130 punches, thuds and impacts.

## Fonts

- **Title and HUD text:** [Kenney Fonts](../catalog/fonts/kenney-fonts.md). Twelve pixel and display faces.
