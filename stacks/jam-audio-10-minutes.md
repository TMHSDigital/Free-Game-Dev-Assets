# Game jam: all the audio in 10 minutes

For the last evening of a jam, when the game works but is silent. Each pick is a ready-made pack for one need, so there is nothing to search: download, drop the files in, wire them up. None of the picks asks for credit. When a pack lacks one sound, the generator under Tools makes it in seconds.

## Audio

- **Music, retro:** [5 Chiptunes (Action), SubspaceAudio](../catalog/audio/subspaceaudio-5-chiptunes.md). Five looping tracks: Title Screen, Level 1 to 3 and Ending.
- **Music, any style:** [Abstraction Music Loop Bundle](../catalog/audio/tallbeard-abstraction-music-loop-bundle.md). Over 200 loops for games that are not retro.
- **Win, lose and pickup cues:** [Kenney Music Jingles](../catalog/audio/kenney-music-jingles.md). 85 short stingers: level complete, game over, item pickup, menu confirm.
- **Menu clicks:** [Kenney UI Audio](../catalog/audio/kenney-ui-audio.md). 50 clicks and switches for toggles, confirm and cancel.
- **Hits and impacts:** [Kenney Impact Sounds](../catalog/audio/kenney-impact-sounds.md). 130 punches, thuds and physical impacts.
- **Footsteps:** [GboxMikeFozzy Footsteps (OGA)](../catalog/audio/gboxmikefozzy-footsteps.md). A small set of real recorded footsteps.
- **Sci-fi:** [Kenney Sci-fi Sounds](../catalog/audio/kenney-sci-fi-sounds.md). 70 lasers, thrusters and engine sounds.
- **Everything else:** [BigSoundBank](../catalog/audio/bigsoundbank.md). A library of over 3,500 field and foley recordings; read the entry for which files to use.

## Tools

- **The one sound nobody recorded:** [ChipTone](../catalog/tools/chiptone.md). A sound-effect generator in the browser that exports WAV.
